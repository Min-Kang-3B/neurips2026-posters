# NeurIPS 2026 posters (WebP mirror)

Compressed WebP copies (long edge 1400px) of NeurIPS 2026 posters as published on
neurips.cc (`/media/PosterPDFs/NeurIPS 2026/<paper_id>.png`), used by the unofficial
schedule site https://min-kang-3b.github.io/neurips2026-schedule/ .

Posters are the work of their authors. Originals: `https://neurips.cc/virtual/2026/poster/<paper_id>`.
Authors who want a poster removed can open an issue.
